# Luma — DESIGN.md

Outcome-only design reference for the recreated Luma UI.

## Surface
White canvas, near-black ink, subtle grey sheets.

## Brand gradient
Pink #FF5A7E → amber #FFB84D on the promo wall and start-here text.

## Detail page
Cover-derived dark background (deep navy), white hero card, pill buttons.

## Components
Date-grouped feed cards, horizontal popular rail, category icon chips, floating Create Event capsule, four bottom tabs.
